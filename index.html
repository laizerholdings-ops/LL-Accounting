Core Tax & Statutory Services

Stay completely compliant with South African Revenue Service (SARS) regulations. We eliminate the stress of tax deadlines by managing all filings accurately on your behalf.

  • Company Income Tax Submissions We prepare and submit corporate tax returns efficiently, reconcile your financial records against SARS assessments, and help you optimize deductions legally to minimize tax liabilities.
  • Provisional Tax Calculations & Returns Accurate calculation and timely submission of first and second period provisional tax returns (IRP6), preventing costly underestimation penalties and cash-flow surprises.
  • Individual Tax Compliance Personal income tax return (ITR12) submissions for sole proprietors, freelancers, corporate executives, and private individuals, ensuring all medical, travel, and retirement deductions are claimed.
  • VAT Returns & Registrations Bi-monthly or monthly Value-Added Tax (VAT201) calculation, audit-ready summaries, electronic submissions, and fast-track new VAT vendor registrations with SARS.
  • PAYE, UIF, Payslips & IRP5s Complete monthly payroll administration including EMP201 submissions, professional employee payslips, UIF declarations, and bi-annual EMP501 reconciliation with IRP5 certificate issuance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our tax submissions, company registrations, and consulting services.

What documents do I need for my provisional tax submission?

You typically need your bank statements for the relevant tax period, sales invoices, expense receipts, asset purchase records, and prior year assessment notices. Leon will provide you with a comprehensive checklist tailored to your business structure.

How long does a new company registration take with CIPC?

New private company (Pty) Ltd registrations usually take between 2 to 5 business days, depending on CIPC name approval and director verification turnaround times. We handle the entire submission process for you.

Do you offer remote accounting services outside Jeffreys Bay?

Yes! While we are physically based in Paradise Beach and Jeffreys Bay, we utilize secure cloud accounting tools like QuickBooks to assist clients across South Africa remotely.
